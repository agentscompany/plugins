# Shopee

Business insights and products in Seller Centre, in the agent's browser, signed in to your account.

## How it connects

The agent's own Chromium profile. You sign in yourself in the agent's browser (you take over the screen). Agents read pages as text and act by element number.

## Permissions

Your seller session in the agent's browser

## What agents can do

- Read business insights (sales, visitors, conversion, product ranking)
- Suggest listing improvements
- Edit products (after you approve)

## What agents don't do

- Type your password or solve verifications
- Change prices or stock without your approval

Anything that changes something on your behalf (sending, publishing, editing, approving) needs your confirmation first.
