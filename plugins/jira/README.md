# Jira

Agents see the tickets assigned to you, comment and move their status, through acli, Atlassian's official CLI.

## How it connects

`acli`, installed on the workspace computer (PC). Login: OAuth in your browser, then pick the Jira site.

## Permissions

acli OAuth (read/write Jira work)

## What agents can do

- Search your open tickets (JQL)
- Read tickets and comments
- Comment and transition status (after you confirm)

## What agents don't do

- Act on your behalf without asking first

Anything that changes something on your behalf (sending, publishing, editing, approving) needs your confirmation first.
